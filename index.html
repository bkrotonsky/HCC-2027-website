The Competition

What HCC27 Asks of Us

The Hydropower Collegiate Competition is a Department of Energy–backed challenge that gives student teams roughly eight months, from the fall kickoff through a spring industry event, to take a real hydropower opportunity from a rough idea to a defensible engineering package, with mentorship from industry professionals along the way.

01

Pick a Site

We select a site category (a non-powered dam, a pumped storage opportunity, or an in-conduit system) and assess whether it's a realistic candidate for hydropower.

02

Design It

Once a site is chosen, we produce a conceptual design for the facility or a deep-dive design for one of its key components.

03

Connect & Present

We engage industry and community stakeholders throughout, then bring the season together in a poster, a quick pitch, and a final report at the national event.

Our Goals

What Success Looks Like for Our Team

Beyond the competition score, these are the three goals we're holding ourselves to this season.

01

Choose a Site That Matters

Select the NPD that is feasible and would have considerable impact on the surrounding community.

02

Build Industry Relationships

Build industry relationships to gain connections and improve as engineers.

03

Inspire the Next Generation

Improve our community by getting K-12 students involved and excited about hydropower.
