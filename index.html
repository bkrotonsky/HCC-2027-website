The Competition

What HCC27 Asks of Us

The Hydropower Collegiate Competition is a Department of Energy–backed challenge that gives student teams roughly eight months — from the fall kickoff through a spring industry event — to take a real hydropower opportunity from a rough idea to a defensible engineering package, with mentorship from industry professionals along the way.

01

Pick a Site

We select a site category — a non-powered dam, a pumped storage opportunity, or an in-conduit system — and assess whether it's a realistic candidate for hydropower.

02

Design It

Once a site is chosen, we produce a conceptual design for the facility or a deep-dive design for one of its key components.

03

Connect & Present

We engage industry and community stakeholders throughout, then bring the season together in a poster, a quick pitch, and a final report at the national event.

What We're Doing This Season

This page will be updated as our site selection and scope firm up. In broad strokes, our capstone team is spending the fall semester on research and site assessment, and the spring semester translating that assessment into a complete design package.

Our Goals

What Success Looks Like for Our Team

Beyond the competition score, these are the goals we're holding ourselves to this season — placeholders for now, to be refined as the team finalizes its project charter.

01

Deliver a Defensible Design

Produce a siting and design package that could credibly move toward a real next step, not just a competition submission.

02

Build Industry Relationships

Use the Community Connections challenge to make real contacts in the hydropower industry, not a checkbox exercise.

03

Grow as Engineers

Apply and stress-test our coursework on a project with real constraints, stakeholders, and deadlines.

04

Represent NAU Well

Put together a presentation and prototype (if time allows) that we're proud to bring to the national event on NAU's behalf.
