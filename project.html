Project Breakdown

Our
Subprojects

HCC27 breaks down into three main scored subprojects plus two required unscored deliverables and one optional stretch challenge. Click any section below for details and its associated documents.

Scored · 35%

Siting

Selecting and assessing the feasibility of a hydropower site for HCC27.

The Task

Choosing a Site Category

Our team is selecting one site category to pursue for the competition — placeholder pending final selection:

  • New Powered Dam (NPD) Conversion — 1–10 MW
  • Pumped Storage Hydropower (PSH) System Integration — up to 1 GW
  • In-Conduit System — 100 kW–10 MW

Once a site is chosen, we'll build a full feasibility assessment covering the technical, environmental, and economic factors that determine whether the project could realistically move forward.

Scored · 40%

Design

Producing a conceptual design for the selected site or one of its components.

Two Tracks

Choosing a Design Track

Placeholder pending team decision — we'll pursue one of two design tracks:

  • Facility Conceptual Design — a full conceptual design for the selected hydropower site
  • Component Deep Dive — a final design package for one individual component of the site

As the highest-weighted challenge in the competition, this is where our team will spend the bulk of its engineering effort, translating the siting assessment into a concrete, defensible design package.

Scored · 25%

Community Connections

Engaging industry professionals to connect, create, and commit to a solution.

Three Goals

Connect, Create, Commit

  • Connect with professionals to discuss an industry challenge
  • Create unique solutions to address that challenge
  • Commit to one of the solutions and complete it

We believe innovative solutions are built through collaboration — this challenge keeps industry and community engagement a core, scored part of our project rather than an afterthought.

Required · Unscored · 100 pts

Design & Siting Poster

A summary poster of our design and siting work, presented at the final event.

Details

Summarizing the Season

We'll create and bring a poster to the final event that distills the siting assessment and design work into a single, presentable summary for judges and attendees.

Required · Unscored

Quick Pitch

A 90-second pitch communicating our biggest takeaways from the project.

Format

90 Seconds, No Points

Every team delivers a 90-second pitch summarizing the project's biggest takeaways. Attendees at the final event vote on their favorites — a chance to communicate clearly, not to chase a score.

Optional

Build & Test

Building and testing a scaled prototype of our concept or powerhouse.

Stretch Goal

From Concept to Prototype

If time and resources allow, our team will build a scaled physical prototype of our design and run it through a series of tests — a hands-on validation of the design decisions made earlier in the season.
